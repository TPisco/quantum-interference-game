Phase Shift

Five quantum puzzles about interference. Turn invisible phases into light and dark, then confirm your circuit on IonQ.

Five puzzles

Each solved puzzle opens the next. Earn up to three stars per puzzle.

How it works

Split
H (Hadamard) sends a qubit down two paths at once: the upper path |0⟩ and the lower path |1⟩.
Rotate
A phase gate P(φ) turns the lower path's amplitude arrow by φ. Its length never changes.
Measure
An outcome's probability is the squared length of its arrow. A measurement cannot see which way an arrow points.
Interfere
When paths meet on one outcome, their arrows add head to tail. Aligned arrows reinforce, opposite arrows cancel.

Stars

  • Solve the puzzle.

  • Confirm your solution on IonQ.

        Why? Follow the arrows
        
            
        Each path that reaches an outcome carries an amplitude arrow. Quantum rule: add the arrows, then square the length. Classical rule: square each arrow, then add. The difference is interference.
