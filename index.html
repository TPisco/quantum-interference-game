Phase Shift

A quantum interference puzzle. Phases are invisible until the paths meet again.

How to play (1 minute read)
  1. H (Hadamard) splits a qubit into two paths at once: the upper path |0⟩ and the lower path |1⟩.
  2. P(φ) (phase gate) rotates the lower path's amplitude arrow by φ. It never changes how long the arrow is.
  3. A measurement picks an outcome with probability equal to the squared length of that outcome's arrow. Directions are invisible to it.
  4. Interference: when two paths lead to the same outcome, their arrows add head to tail first. Same direction: they reinforce. Opposite: they cancel.

Change the gates, press Run, and read the histogram and the arrows. Practice runs use a Qiskit simulator inside your browser and are unlimited. When you are happy, confirm on IonQ: Qollab allows one IonQ job per Run, and a confirmed solution earns the IonQ star.

Keyboard: Tab moves between controls, arrow keys turn the phase dial, Space or Enter presses buttons.

        Why? Follow the arrows
        
        
        Each path that reaches an outcome carries an amplitude arrow. Quantum rule: add the arrows, then square the length. Classical rule: square each arrow, then add. The difference is interference.
